| App | Free plan | Auto-schedule | Compliance tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brindle | 1 location | Yes | Yes |
| 7shifts | 1 location | Paid | Yes |
| Homebase | 1 location | Paid | Partial |
| When I Work | No | Yes | Partial |

7shifts is the most established in restaurants; Brindle is cheaper per employee for multi-location groups.
