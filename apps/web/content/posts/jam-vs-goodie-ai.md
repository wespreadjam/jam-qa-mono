**Jam and Goodie AI both track how often your brand is cited in AI answers, but they differ in what happens next.** Goodie is an answer engine optimization platform with broad engine coverage (up to 14 models), attribution and SOC 2, starting at [$295 per month](https://higoodie.com/pricing). Jam tracks six engines, then ships articles as pull requests and runs cold email outreach from the same product, with [$299 per month shown at 400 tracked prompts](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Pick Goodie for enterprise coverage and revenue attribution. Pick Jam if you want one tool that monitors, writes and distributes.

## Which tool tracks more AI engines?

Goodie does. Its Core plan tracks [5 models, Pro 8 and Enterprise up to 14](https://higoodie.com/pricing). Goodie lists [ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overview, AI Mode, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, Copilot, Alexa for Shopping, Sparky and Muse](https://higoodie.com/blog/best-aeo-software-tools) in its own roundup.

Jam tracks [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) on every plan. Jam's pricing page does not list Claude, Grok, DeepSeek or Meta AI. If your buyers use those assistants, or you sell through AI shopping surfaces, Goodie covers more.

Jam's Visibility Score is the [share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://www.spreadjam.com/). Its gap analysis [shows where you are not being cited](https://www.spreadjam.com/) across platforms.

## How do Jam and Goodie AI pricing compare?

The two price points are close at the entry level and work differently after that.

**Goodie AI** sells fixed tiers: [Core at $295 per month, Pro at $999 per month, and custom Enterprise pricing](https://higoodie.com/pricing). Core includes a 7-day trial, 110 prompts, one brand and 50 optimizations a month. Goodie's pricing page offers [annual billing with 2 months free](https://higoodie.com/pricing).

**Jam** uses a plan builder. You choose from [50, 200, 400, 600, 800 or 1,000 tracked prompts](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). The page shows [$299 per month at 400 prompts, scanned every other day, with 2,000 work credits](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Jam does not publish prices for the other slider positions on that page. Work credits pay for output: [an article costs about 150 credits, a deep research report about 50 and a chat task 10 to 40](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). So 2,000 credits covers about 13 articles. [Top-ups never expire](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

The practical difference at the entry level: [Goodie Core at $295 gives you 110 prompts tracked daily](https://higoodie.com/pricing), while [Jam at $299 shows 400 prompts scanned every other day plus credits to write articles](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

## Which one helps you create and publish content?

Both products go beyond monitoring.

Goodie's [Optimization Actions give a prioritized playbook of what to fix](https://higoodie.com/), and its Content Studio produces content in your brand voice. NoGood's review says the writer is [currently focused on blog creation](https://nogood.io/blog/goodie-ai-review). Goodie also exports tasks to Jira, according to [its own roundup](https://higoodie.com/blog/best-aeo-software-tools).

Jam writes [researched articles and ships them as pull requests](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing), plans content, and connects to [Google Search Console](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Teams can [approve drafts and review PRs from Slack and GitHub](https://www.spreadjam.com/). Every Jam plan includes the full product, so nothing is held back for a higher tier.

## Which one does cold email outreach?

Jam runs outreach inside the product. It offers [one inbox for every account](https://www.spreadjam.com/), lead enrichment that turns a name into a profile, and auto warm-up so [mailboxes ramp to stay out of spam](https://www.spreadjam.com/). It also lets you send straight from Gmail, per its [changelog entry of May 13, 2026](https://www.spreadjam.com/).

Goodie has a narrower outreach feature. NoGood's review describes an Outreach Agent that [drafts personalized emails to authoritative publishers and sources](https://nogood.io/blog/goodie-ai-review). That is aimed at earning citations, not at running a prospecting inbox.

## Which one is better for enterprise and agencies?

Goodie. Its Enterprise plan lists [up to 14 models, multi-brand performance, MCP and API access, SOC 2 compliance and a dedicated AEO strategist](https://higoodie.com/pricing). Its homepage describes [multi-domain dashboards and white-label reporting for agencies](https://higoodie.com/). It also offers [attribution tied to GA4 and Adobe](https://higoodie.com/blog/best-aeo-software-tools) and an agentic commerce suite for retail.

Jam offers custom terms for [more than 1,000 prompts](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Its pages do not list SOC 2 or an agency program.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Goodie AI if you:**
- Need coverage beyond six engines, such as Claude, Grok, DeepSeek or Meta AI.
- Sell products through AI shopping and want SKU-level visibility on [Pro or Enterprise](https://higoodie.com/pricing).
- Need SOC 2, multi-brand reporting or revenue attribution.
- Want daily data on every tier.

**Pick Jam if you:**
- Want monitoring, article production and outreach in one product.
- Prefer to size the plan by prompt count and pay for work done in credits.
- Are a founder or small growth team that wants content delivered as pull requests.
- Want a larger prompt count at the entry price: [400 prompts at $299](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) against [110 on Goodie Core at $295](https://higoodie.com/pricing).

## FAQ

**Is Jam a Goodie AI alternative?**
Yes, for teams whose main goal is getting cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Copilot. Both track visibility and competitor gaps. Jam adds outreach and PR-based publishing. Goodie adds wider engine coverage and attribution.

**How much does Goodie AI cost?**
[Core is $295 per month, Pro is $999 per month, and Enterprise is custom](https://higoodie.com/pricing).

**How much does Jam cost?**
Jam's pricing page shows [$299 per month at 400 tracked prompts](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing), and you can [start free](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Larger needs go through a call.

**Does Goodie AI replace Ahrefs or Semrush?**
No. NoGood's review says it [layers on top of Ahrefs, Semrush and GA4](https://nogood.io/blog/goodie-ai-review), and that it lacks site audits, keyword explorers and backlink crawlers. Jam includes [SEO data analysis for keywords, SERP rankings and traffic](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

**Can I try each before paying?**
Goodie Core has a [7-day free trial](https://higoodie.com/pricing). Jam's call to action is [Start free](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).
