---
title: How to make a restaurant staff schedule (step by step)
description: Forecast, set coverage, collect availability, then publish early.
date: 2026-10-03
---

1. **Forecast covers** by hour from last year's sales and upcoming events.
2. **Set coverage targets** per role: servers, line cooks, hosts.
3. **Collect availability** at least two weeks ahead.
4. **Publish early** and lock swaps 24 hours before each shift.
