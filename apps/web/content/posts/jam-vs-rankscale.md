**Rankscale is a measurement platform: it tracks your brand across [17+ AI engines](https://rankscale.ai/pricing) and gives you deep analytics. Jam tracks [six engines](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) and then does the follow-up work: it writes articles and ships them as pull requests, and it runs outreach ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).** Pick Rankscale if you need the widest engine coverage, sentiment and shopping data, or agency reporting. Pick Jam if your bottleneck is turning gaps into published pages and replies.

## How do Jam and Rankscale differ on pricing?

Rankscale publishes fixed tiers. Pro is $99 a month with 1,200 credits and 10 brand dashboards. Pro+ is $229 with 3,000 credits. Growth is $385 with 5,500 credits. Enterprise is $780 with 12,000 credits ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)). The same page also shows lower per-month figures ($84, $195, $327 and $663) next to those prices without labelling the billing cycle, so confirm which one applies at checkout.

Essentials is a smaller entry plan at $20 a month ($17 shown as the lower figure) with 120 credits, 2 brand dashboards and up to 480 answers. It is yearly only and has no credit rollover ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)). Third-party sources disagree on whether it is still sold: [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/rankscale-ai-visibility-platform) notes that sources disagree, and an [OMR review](https://omr.com/en/reviews/product/rankscale-ai) lists the removal of the cheapest subscription as a drawback for small businesses.

Jam's pricing page shows no dollar prices. It says "Start free," that every plan gets the full product, and that standard plans cover 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts. Above that, it builds a custom plan ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Work is paid from one credit balance: an article costs about 150 credits, a deep research report about 50, and a chat task about 10 to 40. The page says 2,000 credits covers about 13 articles.

The two models count different things, so a price comparison needs care. Rankscale charges to query engines, [typically 0.25 credits per engine per prompt](https://rankscale.ai/pricing). Jam [charges for work done](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), including content and research.

## Which AI engines does each tool track?

Rankscale lists "17+" engines on every plan. Its homepage names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Copilot, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI Grok and Meta's Muse Spark. About 11 of the 17+ are named anywhere on the page ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/)).

Jam tracks six: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). If you need Claude, DeepSeek, Grok or Mistral visibility, Rankscale covers more of them.

## What do you do after the dashboard shows a gap?

This is the main difference. Rankscale's feature list covers visibility scores, competitor analysis, citation and sources analysis, sentiment, shopping and ads, query fanouts, page audits and prompt research. Its recommendations feature is labelled beta ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/)). Page audits check AI bot crawlability and return fixes, but the feature list has no article writing or outreach.

Jam's pricing page lists competitor gap analysis, content planning, articles "researched, written and shipped as pull requests," deep research reports, and a chat agent with 50+ tools ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Its homepage adds a unified inbox, lead enrichment, automatic mailbox warm-up and custom automations, and says you can approve drafts and review PRs from Slack and GitHub ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)). A written article still needs your review before it goes live, since it arrives as a pull request.

## Where is Rankscale stronger?

- **Engine coverage.** 17+ engines against Jam's six ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/pricing), [Jam](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).
- **Analytics depth.** Sentiment by brand, topic and model, shopping and ad placements, query fanouts and estimated prompt volume ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/)).
- **Scheduling.** Hourly scans are available ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)); Jam's fastest published cadence is daily ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).
- **Agencies.** Growth adds white-labelled dashboard links, client discounts, affiliate commissions and a partner directory listing ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)).
- **Developer access.** REST API from Pro+ and MCP on every plan ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)).
- **Published prices.** You can budget before talking to anyone.

Reviewers also flag drawbacks. An [OMR review summary](https://omr.com/en/reviews/product/rankscale-ai) names a steep learning curve, says the credit system quickly limits users on the smallest package, and says reporting for end clients is limited, although the raw KPI data is solid. One reviewer there also says sentiment interpretation does not always feel accurate, especially negative sentiment.

## Where is Jam stronger?

- **Closing the loop.** Findings turn into drafted articles delivered as pull requests, in the same product that measures them ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).
- **Outreach.** Jam includes an inbox, enrichment and warm-up ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)). Rankscale's feature list shows none of these ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/)).
- **One balance.** A single credit balance pays for articles, research and chat tasks, and top-ups never expire ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

Jam's gaps are plain. Its pricing page shows no dollar prices and no trial terms, it tracks six engines rather than 17+, and its homepage defines its visibility score only as "share of tracked prompts where you are cited" without a method ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).

## FAQ

**Is Jam a Rankscale alternative?**
Partly. Both track brand visibility in AI answers. Jam adds article shipping and outreach; Rankscale adds more engines and deeper analytics.

**How much does Rankscale cost?**
Pro is $99 a month, Pro+ $229, Growth $385 and Enterprise $780. Essentials is $20 a month, yearly only ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)).

**How much does Jam cost?**
Jam says "Start free" and its pricing page shows no dollar prices. Articles cost about 150 credits and deep research reports about 50 ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

**Does Rankscale write content?**
Its feature list covers page audits and recommended fixes, not article writing ([Rankscale](https://rankscale.ai/)).

**Does Jam track Claude?**
Jam's pricing page lists six engines and Claude is not among them ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

**Does Rankscale have a free trial?**
It shows a "Try Pro for Free" button. The trial length is not stated on the pricing page ([Rankscale pricing](https://rankscale.ai/pricing)).
