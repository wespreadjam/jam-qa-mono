## What happens after you find a citation gap?

Finding a gap is the easy part. The harder question is what your workflow looks like once you know a competitor is being cited and you are not.

With a monitoring-only tool, the answer is: you export the data and figure out the next step yourself. That usually means briefing a writer, publishing new content, and hoping the AI engines pick it up before your next scan.

Jam closes that loop inside the same platform, as documented in the [Jam changelog](https://spreadjam.com/changelog):

- The SEO agent audits your site for citability and identifies where you are missing from AI search results.
- The content engine drafts a structured outline, writes against it, runs the result through a prose linter and a second model critique, then ships the article as a pull request, complete with structured data that AI assistants use to cite sources.
- Jam remembers what it has already published for you and uses that history to fill gaps rather than repeat itself.
- When a workflow step fails, Jam diagnoses and retries it before giving up.

The result is a single workflow that covers both search rankings and AI citations, rather than separate tools for each.
