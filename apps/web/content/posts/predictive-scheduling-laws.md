| Jurisdiction | Notice required | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| New York City | 14 days | Fast food, retail |
| Chicago | 14 days | Several industries, 100+ employees |
| Seattle | 14 days | Food service and retail, 500+ employees |
| Oregon | 14 days | Retail, hospitality, food, 500+ employees |

Penalties usually mean "predictability pay" for each late change.
