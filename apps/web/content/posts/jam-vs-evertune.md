**Jam and Evertune both track how often AI answer engines mention your brand, but they are built for different jobs.** Evertune is a sales-led platform that samples each prompt 100 times per model and turns visibility gaps into paid ChatGPT ads. Jam tracks six engines on every plan and turns gaps into published articles (shipped as pull requests) and cold email outreach. Pick Evertune for statistically benchmarked, enterprise-style reporting and paid media. Pick Jam if you want one credit-based tool that finds citation gaps, ships the content and runs outbound.

## What is the difference between Jam and Evertune?

Evertune describes itself as "the marketing platform for brand discovery in AI search" and claims to be [the only GEO platform that helps advertisers own the entire AI customer journey](https://www.evertune.ai/). Its pipeline runs from prompt research to measurement, then content, and ends in paid retargeting and ChatGPT ads. It reports trust by over 700 advertisers and agencies on its homepage, and a September 2026 note names it a Notable Vendor in Forrester's Answer Engine Optimization Technologies Landscape for Q3 2026.

Jam describes itself as [marketing agents for growth channels](https://www.spreadjam.com/). Its AI search side finds where you are not cited, then publishes articles meant to earn those citations. Its outbound side finds and enriches contacts, warms up mailboxes and triages replies in one inbox. Everything is metered in one credit balance.

The simplest split: Evertune spends your budget to buy visibility where you are missing. Jam spends credits to create the content and outreach that earn it.

## Which AI engines does each tool track?

Jam tracks [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) on every plan. Jam's pricing page does not list Claude, Meta AI or DeepSeek.

Evertune tracks more surfaces, but gates them by plan. According to [its pricing page](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing):

- Search-type models (ChatGPT Search, Gemini Search, AI Mode, AI Overview, Copilot) are available on Starter, Pro and Enterprise.
- Base models (ChatGPT Base, Gemini Base, Meta AI, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek) are available on Pro and Enterprise only.

Evertune tracks Claude, Meta AI and DeepSeek through APIs only, [according to Profound's comparison](https://www.tryprofound.com/articles/profound-vs-evertune). That page is written by a competitor of Evertune, so treat it as one view. If Claude or DeepSeek coverage matters to you, Evertune covers them and Jam's pricing page does not.

## How do they measure AI visibility?

Evertune's method is its headline. It [samples each prompt 100 times per model](https://www.evertune.ai/) and states a margin of error of ±2 points against ±31 points for "typical competitors". These figures are Evertune's own claims. Its Starter plan does not include the 100-sample significance feature; [the compare table](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing) lists "statistical significance from day 1" on Pro and Enterprise only.

Jam reports a [Visibility Score, defined as the share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://www.spreadjam.com/). It runs scans [weekly, every other day or daily](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Jam does not publish a sample count per prompt or a margin of error, so you cannot compare its statistical confidence directly with Evertune's.

## How does pricing compare?

**Evertune** publishes three tiers on [its pricing page](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing):

| Plan | Price | Prompt credits | Models | Ads Agent fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $60 a month | 5,000 a month | 5 | 10% of media |
| Pro | $800 a month, billed annually | 100,000 a month | 11 | 8% of media |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 11 | Custom |

Evertune's credits are not the same as prompts. Its calculator shows the formula: 50 prompts × 1 model × 3 samples a day × 30 days = 4,500 credits a month. So the number of prompts you can track depends on how many models and samples you choose. One third-party review describes Pro as [100,000 tracked prompts](https://ampifire.com/blog/evertune-ai-reviews-pricing-is-this-geo-platform-legit); Evertune's own page counts prompt credits. The same review, published on the blog of a competing content vendor, says Evertune [has no self-serve signup and no advertised free trial](https://ampifire.com/blog/evertune-ai-reviews-pricing-is-this-geo-platform-legit). That predates the self-serve Starter plan now on Evertune's pricing page, which links to app signup.

**Jam** meters work in credits. [Its pricing page](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) says what each task costs: about 150 credits for a researched and written article, about 50 for a deep research report and about 10 to 40 for a chat task. It gives 2,000 credits as covering about 13 articles. Top-ups never expire, you can change plans at any time and historical results are kept when you do. Prompt tracking runs from 50 to 1,000 prompts, and teams needing more, or custom terms, go to Enterprise.

The Jam pricing page does not state dollar prices or plan names, so a like-for-like monthly price comparison is not possible from it. A competitor's review reports [Jam Starter at $39 a month and Pro at $79 per seat](https://www.tryrunlo.com/compare/spreadjam-alternative); treat that as secondhand and confirm current prices with Jam before budgeting.

## What do they do beyond monitoring?

**Content.** Evertune's Content Agent produces [25 articles a month on Pro and unlimited on Enterprise](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing), and its Content Studio tests message variants against AI models, per [Profound's description](https://www.tryprofound.com/articles/profound-vs-evertune). Jam writes articles and [ships them as pull requests](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) to your site repo, and its chat agent has [50+ tools](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

**Paid media.** Evertune's Ads Agent builds and runs ChatGPT ad campaigns from visibility gaps and charges a percentage of media spend. Jam's pricing and home pages do not list a paid advertising feature.

**Outbound.** Jam has [cold email outreach tools](https://www.spreadjam.com/): lead enrichment, automatic mailbox warm-up and a unified inbox. Evertune's pricing and home pages list no email outreach feature.

**Enterprise controls.** Evertune's Enterprise plan adds [dedicated support, Fact Checker, AI website optimization, data exports and SSO](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing). Jam's pricing page does not list SSO or compliance certifications.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Evertune if:**

- You are a larger brand or agency that wants every prompt sampled 100 times per model and a stated margin of error.
- You want paid ChatGPT ads or programmatic retargeting driven by visibility data.
- You need Claude, Meta AI or DeepSeek tracked, or SSO and data export to your own cloud.
- You are comfortable with a demo-led buying process on the higher tiers.

**Pick Jam if:**

- You are a founder or small growth team that wants one tool for visibility tracking, content shipped to your repo and cold outreach.
- You work in Slack and GitHub and want to approve drafts and pull requests there.
- You prefer paying per task through credits over percent-of-media fees.
- Your priority engines are ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Copilot.

**Use both if** you want Evertune's benchmarking and ads alongside Jam's content and outreach. Their feature sets overlap mainly on tracking and article creation.

## FAQ

### Is Jam an alternative to Evertune?

Partly. Both track AI answer engines and create content. Evertune adds paid ads and large-scale sampling. Jam adds cold email outreach and publishing through pull requests.

### Does Evertune have a free trial?

Evertune's [pricing page](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing) shows a 7-day free trial of the Ads Manager Agent on Starter. It does not show a free trial of visibility tracking.

### How much does Evertune cost?

Starter is $60 a month and Pro is $800 a month billed annually. Enterprise is custom, per [Evertune's pricing page](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing).

### How much does Jam cost?

Jam's [pricing page](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) says "Start free" and lists what work costs in credits, such as about 150 credits for an article. It does not publish dollar prices.

### Which tool tracks more AI engines?

Evertune tracks up to 11 models on Pro and Enterprise. Jam tracks six engines on every plan.
