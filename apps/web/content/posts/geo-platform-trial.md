A GEO platform trial lets you see how AI answers mention your brand before you pay.

## What you get

- Tracked prompts across several AI engines
- A first scan of your visibility

## How long does setup take — and when do you see first data?

Setting up Jam takes under 30 minutes from account creation to your first tracked scan. How quickly you see actionable citation data depends on the scan cadence you choose.

### Four steps to your first visibility report

1. **Create your account and add your domain** — enter your brand name and website URL. This takes about two minutes.
2. **Build your prompt set** — add the questions your buyers ask AI engines. Start with 10–20 prompts covering your category, use cases, and key competitors. Jam suggests prompts based on your domain to speed this up.
3. **Select your AI engines** — choose from ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Copilot, and Perplexity. You can track all of them or focus on the engines your audience uses most.
4. **Run your first scan** — Jam queries every engine with every prompt and records whether your brand is cited, where in the answer it appears, and which competitors are named alongside you.

Total time from sign-up to completed first scan: **under 30 minutes**.

### When does actionable data land?

The table below shows when you can expect to spot your first visibility gaps, based on the scan cadence you set.

| Scan cadence | First scan completes | First trend comparison available | Recommended for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Within 24 hours of setup | After week 2 | Teams monitoring a stable prompt set |
| Every other day | Within 24 hours of setup | After day 4 | Teams running active GEO experiments |
| Daily | Within 24 hours of setup | After day 2 | Teams in competitive categories or launching content |

All cadences deliver your first scan on the same day you finish setup. The difference is how quickly you accumulate enough data points to compare performance over time and identify which prompts are moving.

### What "actionable visibility gaps" looks like in practice

After your first scan you can see:

- Which of your tracked prompts mention a competitor but not your brand
- Which AI engines cite you most and least often
- Which prompts return zero brand citations for anyone — an opportunity to be first

After a second scan (day 2 on daily cadence, day 4 on every-other-day, week 2 on weekly) you can compare runs and see whether a page you published or updated has changed your citation rate.
