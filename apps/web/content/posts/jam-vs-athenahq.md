Last updated: November 2026

**Short answer:** Pick AthenaHQ if you want a dedicated AI search dashboard with broad model coverage (11 models on its [Starter plan](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing)), a published price of $295 per month, and enterprise controls such as SSO and BI dashboards. Pick Jam if you want to go from a citation gap to a shipped article and to outreach in one workspace: Jam tracks six answer engines and [ships articles as pull requests](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), and its [homepage](https://spreadjam.com/) describes a built-in inbox for email outreach. Jam does not publish dollar prices, so cost has to be compared by usage, not by sticker price.

## How much do Jam and AthenaHQ cost?

AthenaHQ lists Starter at [$295 per month](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing) and Enterprise as custom. Its pricing page does not show a price for the Essential plan, an annual price, or prices for the API and extra-credit add-ons ("Contact us"). Cognizo's review reports a [17 percent annual discount](https://www.cognizo.ai/blog/athenahq-review). Some third-party pages quote a range of [about $295 to $499 per month and no free trial](https://www.rankability.com/blog/athenahq-ai-review). That conflicts with AthenaHQ's own page, which lists an Essential plan, so treat the higher figure as unconfirmed.

Jam's [pricing page](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) lists no plan names or dollar prices. It says every plan gets the full product, covers 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts, and sends customers who need more than 1,000 prompts to custom terms. Credit top-ups never expire, and you can change plan at any time.

## How is usage metered?

The two products charge for different things.

- **AthenaHQ charges per AI response.** One credit is one response. Starter's [3,600 monthly credits](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing) cover about 10 prompts checked daily across all 11 models (10 prompts x 11 models x 30 days = 3,300 credits). Tracking more prompts or running more frequent checks uses credits faster, and extra credits are a paid add-on.
- **Jam charges per piece of work.** An [article is about 150 credits, a deep research report about 50, and a chat task about 10 to 40](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Jam's page gives one example: 2,000 credits covers about 13 articles.

If you mainly want monitoring at scale, AthenaHQ's per-response model is easy to size. If you want finished articles, Jam's per-work model shows the cost of each deliverable.

## Which AI engines does each tool track?

AthenaHQ covers more models. Its [homepage](https://www.athenahq.ai/) lists ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI and Mistral, with more available on request. Its Essential plan covers [five of these](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing): ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot.

Jam tracks [six: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). It does not list Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI or Mistral. If those models matter to your buyers, AthenaHQ has the edge.

## Which one helps you act on citation gaps?

Both products try to close the loop between finding a gap and fixing it.

- **Jam** finds [where you are not cited](https://spreadjam.com/), then writes articles that are [researched, written and shipped as pull requests](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Its Visibility Score is the share of tracked prompts where you are cited. You can approve drafts and review PRs from Slack and GitHub.
- **AthenaHQ** offers [citation source analysis, content gap analysis, AI-friendly templates and a content optimization agent](https://www.athenahq.ai/), plus [on-page and off-page actions and a smart robots.txt and llms.txt manager](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing). Cognizo's review notes that AthenaHQ's core value is [visibility into AI answers](https://www.cognizo.ai/blog/athenahq-review), and that teams who need published content may need a separate writing and publishing step. AthenaHQ's own pages describe recommendations and an optimization agent, and do not describe a publish-as-pull-request workflow.

## Does either tool run outreach?

Jam does. Its [homepage](https://spreadjam.com/) describes one inbox for every mailbox, reply triage, lead enrichment that turns a name into a profile, mailbox auto warm-up and custom automations. AthenaHQ's [homepage](https://www.athenahq.ai/) describes PR press kits, real-time brand mention alerts and crisis detection, but no email outreach inbox. Jam does not publish sending limits or deliverability figures.

## Integrations, security and enterprise features

AthenaHQ is stronger here. Its [pricing page](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing) lists Shopify, Webflow, GA4 and Google Search Console integrations. Enterprise adds SAML and OIDC SSO, an audit log, multi-region and multi-language tracking, persona targeting, BI dashboards for Tableau, Power BI and Looker, API access and a 2-hour SLA. Cognizo's review reports [SOC 2 Type 2 and GDPR compliance](https://www.cognizo.ai/blog/athenahq-review) for AthenaHQ.

Jam's published integrations are [Google Search Console](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), Slack, GitHub and Gmail. Its pages do not list SSO, audit logs or compliance certifications.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick AthenaHQ if:**
- You need coverage of Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI or Mistral.
- You run ecommerce and want Shopify and GA4 attribution.
- Your security review requires SSO, audit logs or a compliance report.
- You want a published starting price of $295 per month.

**Pick Jam if:**
- You want articles written and opened as pull requests, not only recommendations.
- You want cold email outreach in the same workspace as your visibility data.
- Your engines of interest are ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Copilot.
- You are a growth engineer or founder who works in Slack and GitHub.

## FAQ

**Is AthenaHQ free?**
AthenaHQ lists an Essential plan without a price on its [pricing page](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing). Cognizo's review says it includes [300 credits](https://www.cognizo.ai/blog/athenahq-review). Starter is $295 per month.

**Does Jam have a free plan?**
Jam's pricing page is headed ["Start free"](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) but does not state the limits of the free offer.

**Does AthenaHQ write content?**
AthenaHQ lists a [content optimization agent and content recommendations](https://www.athenahq.ai/). Its pages do not describe publishing articles as pull requests.

**How many prompts can I track?**
Jam allows [50 to 1,000 on standard plans](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). AthenaHQ Starter has unlimited prompts, but each AI response uses one of [3,600 monthly credits](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).

**Can I use both?**
Yes. AthenaHQ can serve as a broad model monitor while Jam handles content and outreach, though the two products overlap on tracking.
